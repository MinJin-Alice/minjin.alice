Action 4 Focus on the core #

focus on frequency language

Week 1 ： The tool box: ‘What is this?’ ‘How do you say that?‘‘I don’t understand.’ use as tools

Week 2: Pronouns, Common Verbs, Adjectives ‘you’‘me’’that’‘give’‘hot’‘cold’

Week 3 & 4: Glue words ‘and’‘but’’even though’‘although’’therefore’ logical transformer

Action 5 Get a language parent #

  • Does not correct mistakes
  • Confirms understanding by using correct language
  • Uses words the learner knows
