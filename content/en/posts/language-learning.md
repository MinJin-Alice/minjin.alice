# 5 principles

### Principle 1 Focus on language content that is relevant to you

### Principle 2 Use new languages as a tool to communicate from day 1, like a baby
 
### Principle 3 Comprehension is the key

### Principle 4 Language learning is about physiological training, it's about muscles

### Principle 5 be happy and relaxed, and must learn to tolerate ambiguity

# 7 actions

### Action 1 Listen A LOT

brain soaking

### Action 2 focus on body language

### Action 3 Start mixing

speak like a baby: get creative, do not have to be perfect

### Action 4 Focus on the core

focus on frequency language

Week 1 ： The tool box: 'What is this?' 'How do you say that?''I don't understand.' use as tools

Week 2: Pronouns, Common Verbs, Adjectives 'you''me''that''give''hot''cold'

Week 3 & 4: Glue words 'and''but''even though''although''therefore' logical transformer

### Action 5 Get a language parent

- works to understand what you are saying
- Does not correct mistakes
- Confirms understanding by using correct language
- Uses words the learner knows

### Action 6 Copy the face

observe a native speaker's face
learn about the rules of pronounciation
