Kazuma Polyglot — Language Learning Notes

> Source: Summarized from reviews and discussions of Kazuma's book *ゼロから12ヵ国語マスターした私の最強の外国語習得法* (not the original copyrighted text).

Core Idea

Instead of memorizing thousands of isolated vocabulary words, memorize **around 30 high-frequency conversational sentences**. These sentences become reusable patterns that allow you to communicate immediately and naturally.

The objective is **communication first, perfection later**.

---

Basic Conversational Phrases

| Purpose | Example Sentence |
|---------|------------------|
| Greeting | Hello. |
| First meeting | Nice to meet you. |
| Introduce yourself | My name is ____. |
| Ask someone's name | What's your name? |
| Nationality | Where are you from? |
| Languages | Can you speak English? |
| Occupation | What do you do? |
| Current action | What are you doing? |
| Motivation | Why are you studying Japanese? |
| Your reason | I'm studying Japanese as a hobby. |
| Duration | How long have you been studying? |
| Permission | May I do this? |
| Likes | I like Japanese food. |
| Preferences | What's your favorite movie? |
| Plans | What are you doing tomorrow? |
| Experience | Have you ever been to Japan? |
| Recommendation | Can you recommend something? |
| Clarification | Could you say that again? |
| Understanding | I understand. / I don't understand. |
| Gratitude | Thank you very much. |

Learning Tips

1. Learn Complete Sentences

Don't memorize isolated words.

Instead of:

- apple
- eat
- delicious

Learn:

> I like eating apples.

This teaches vocabulary, grammar, and natural word order simultaneously.

---

2. Practice Until Automatic

Repeat each sentence aloud until it can be spoken without thinking.

The goal is to build automatic speaking ability rather than conscious translation.

---

3. Reuse Sentence Patterns

Replace individual words to create dozens of new sentences.

Example:

Original:

> Where are you from?

Derived patterns:

- Where do you live?
- Where do you work?
- Where did you study?
- Where are you going?
- Where is your hometown?

One sentence pattern can generate many useful conversations.

---

4. Start Speaking Early

Don't wait until you "know enough."

Begin talking with native speakers as soon as possible, even if your vocabulary is very limited.

Real conversations provide context and reinforce what you've learned.

---

5. Let Grammar Grow Naturally

Rather than mastering grammar before speaking:

1. Learn useful sentences.
2. Use them repeatedly.
3. Notice the grammar patterns.
4. Generalize those patterns naturally.

Grammar becomes easier to understand after repeated exposure.

---

Why This Works

A small collection of high-frequency conversational sentences allows you to:

- introduce yourself
- ask questions
- understand common replies
- keep conversations going
- build confidence from day one

Instead of learning **thousands of words first**, you learn **language patterns** that are immediately useful in real conversations.

---

Key Takeaway

> Communication is the goal—not memorization.

Learn a small set of practical sentence patterns, practice them until they're automatic, then expand them through real conversations with native speakers.
